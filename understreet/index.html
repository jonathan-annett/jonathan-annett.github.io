Your neighbourhood, turned inside out. Streets are tunnels. Buildings are shafts of open sky. Dinner is in a cave. Step on a ▲ pad to surface, where the cars are real and the only way back is a ▼ pad somewhere else.

Using your streets asks for your location once. It is rounded to roughly 100 m and sent only to OpenStreetMap's public map service to fetch nearby streets. Nothing is saved.
