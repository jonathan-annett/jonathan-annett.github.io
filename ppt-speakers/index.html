Speakers are numbered in slide order within each session. Drag the handle to reorder (within or across sessions), click a name to fix it, or use “split” to start a new session here. Each session becomes a folder of numbered placeholder files like Breakout A / Breakout A - 1 - Jane Doe.pptx.

SpeakerScan — names are detected by structure and language, not a fixed list, so unusual and international names are found too. High & Probable are real presenters; Review holds borderline items (award titles, institutions) for a quick human glance.
Previews are an approximate redraw of each slide — enough to confirm the match, not a pixel copy.
