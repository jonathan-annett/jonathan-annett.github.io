VOICE BOX SING-ALONG

A 1980s-style speech synthesiser that sings. Load a music clip and it will try to find the tune and the words, then sing along.

1MUSIC

NO CLIP LOADED. THE DEMO NEEDS NONE.
Stems. Mark the vocals the robot should copy
0.0 S

Play the song or drag through it to find a part, mark its start and end, and add it. Add as many parts as you like: a chorus, a line here and there, half of a duet. Each part's tune and words are found as soon as you add it; only those parts are analysed. HEAR plays one part with the robot, and each part has its own robot volume. Each part is sung with the original singer unless you switch it to replace them. Songs are saved in this browser as you work. Choose several files at once to load time-aligned stems, such as a Suno stem export: the robot copies the stems marked as vocals, and replacing the singer simply leaves those out. With a single mixed file, slow songs with one clear, unprocessed voice in the middle of a stereo mix work best. Everything runs in your browser, and the speech model downloads once.

2WORDS

Same number of words: each keeps its timing. A different number: the words are spread over the part's notes again.

3TIMING

Select an analysed part to line up its syllables with the singer.

Drag a syllable sideways to move it, drag its right edge to change its length, or drag it up or down to change its note. Syllables that touch within a word stay joined: moving one slides the join and its neighbour stretches to fit. Drag a word in the word row to move the whole word. To move a phrase, select a syllable, press SELECT WORD, then add the words before or after it (or shift-click words in the word row), and drag any part of the selection. Tap the waveform to set the cursor, drag it to scroll, and pinch or use the zoom buttons. The faint line is the singer's pitch, and the waveform is their voice split from the music.

4SCORE

Edit any line to fix a note, a time or a word, then press SING. Notes run from C2 to C6, sharps as F#4. Extra notes on a line are slurred on the last syllable.

5DOWNLOAD

Makes a WAV file, or an MP3 if ticked, using the mixer settings and the Play setting above. ROBOTS ONLY keeps the same timing with silence where the music was, ready to line up in an audio editor. Three minutes of song is about 30 MB as WAV, or about 4 MB as MP3. The MP3 encoder downloads the first time you use it.
