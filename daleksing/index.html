VOICE BOX SING-ALONG

A 1980s-style speech synthesiser that sings. Load a music clip and it will try to find the tune and the words, then sing along.

0.0 S

Play the song or drag through it to find a part, mark its start and end, and add it. Add as many parts as you like: a chorus, a line here and there, half of a duet. Only those parts are analysed. Each part is sung with the original singer unless you switch it to replace them. Slow songs with one clear, unprocessed voice in the middle of a stereo mix work best. Everything runs in your browser, and the speech model downloads once.

3SCORE

Edit any line to fix a note, a time or a word, then press SING. Notes run from C2 to C6, sharps as F#4. Extra notes on a line are slurred on the last syllable.
