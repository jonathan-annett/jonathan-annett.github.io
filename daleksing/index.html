VOICE BOX SING-ALONG

A 1980s-style speech synthesiser that sings. Load a music clip and it will try to find the tune and the words, then sing along.

0.0 S

Play the song or drag through it to find a part, mark its start and end, and add it. Add as many parts as you like: a chorus, a line here and there, half of a duet. Only those parts are analysed. HEAR plays one part with the robot, and each part has its own robot volume. Each part is sung with the original singer unless you switch it to replace them. Songs are saved in this browser as you work. Slow songs with one clear, unprocessed voice in the middle of a stereo mix work best. Everything runs in your browser, and the speech model downloads once.

3SCORE

Edit any line to fix a note, a time or a word, then press SING. Notes run from C2 to C6, sharps as F#4. Extra notes on a line are slurred on the last syllable.

4DOWNLOAD

Makes a WAV file using the mixer settings and the Play setting above. ROBOTS ONLY keeps the same timing with silence where the music was, ready to line up in an audio editor. Three minutes of song is about 30 MB.
